### Engineering for human flourishing

**Software should give imagination room to move.**

The best tools fade into the background when we're in flow, while widening the horizon of what we can explore, create, and become. I build at the intersection of engineering, intelligence, and experience design to bring those possibilities closer.

### What I'm building

**Intelligence that extends our reach**
- **[z0intelligence](https://github.com/kvnloo/z0intelligence)** — evidence-backed context, routing, and decision-making for agent systems
- **[AODL](https://github.com/kvnloo/aodl)** — a typed language for expressing and validating agent orchestration · [explore](https://kvnloo.github.io/aodl/)
- **[z0evals](https://github.com/kvnloo/z0evals)** — reproducible research and evaluations for the Zer0 ecosystem
- **[bend-native](https://github.com/kvnloo/bend-native)** — proof-aware development tools for Hermes and Bend

**Experiences that make possibility tangible**
- **[ACE](https://github.com/kvnloo/ace)** — a human-flourishing campus and digital-twin pretotype · [live site](https://kvnloo.github.io/ace/)
- **[Aural](https://github.com/kvnloo/aural)** — a music-listening companion for live PipeWire DSP, AutoEQ, and visualization
- **[OpenPaw](https://github.com/kvnloo/openpaw)** — an early-stage, evidence-first approach to canine behavior and wellbeing
- **[AudioEngine](https://github.com/kvnloo/AudioEngine)** *(2017)* — iOS real-time audio metering and 14-band dual-channel EQ · [demo/docs](https://kvnloo.github.io/AudioEngine/)

**Tools that protect flow**
- **[tmux-agent-fleet](https://github.com/kvnloo/tmux-agent-fleet)** — a fast, zero-daemon command palette for coding agents
- **[.files](https://github.com/kvnloo/.files)** — my Linux workspace, terminal, and audio DSP setup
- **[oss-factory](https://github.com/kvnloo/oss-factory)** — an evidence-driven workflow for contributing to open source
